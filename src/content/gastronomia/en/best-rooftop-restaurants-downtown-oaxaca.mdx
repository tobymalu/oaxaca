In Oaxaca, you don't just watch the sunset; you sip it and taste it. As the sun goes down and paints the green-stone façades gold, there's only one place to be: up high.

Oaxaca's historic center is packed with world-class food. We've picked the 7 must-visit rooftop terraces where the panoramic view competes head-to-head with what's on your plate.

## 1. Pitiona: Signature Cuisine and Unique Views

Led by Chef José Manuel Baños, Pitiona is a must for fine-dining lovers. Its rooftop looks straight at the domes of Santo Domingo.

- **Don't miss:** the noodle soup with quesillo spheres and its experimental cocktails.
- **Vibe:** sophisticated, ideal for a romantic dinner.

## 2. Casa Oaxaca El Restaurante

The classic that put Oaxaca on the international food map. Chef Alejandro Ruiz blends village traditions with contemporary techniques. Its terrace is intimate and in high demand.

- **Don't miss:** the mole negro with turkey breast, and the salsa prepared at your table.
- **Vibe:** traditional elegance, perfect for making an impression.

![Signature Oaxacan mole with sliced breast, sesame seeds, radish and a marigold flower on a black clay plate](../../../assets/images/mole-oaxaqueno-plato-barro-negro-cocina-de-autor.jpg)

## 3. Tierra del Sol

For travelers after authentic flavors from Oaxaca's Mixteca region, with a privileged view of the Ethnobotanical Garden and the historic center. Chef Olga Cabrera revives ancestral recipes with flawless presentation.

- **Don't miss:** the mole de caderas (in season) and its artisanal bread.
- **Vibe:** authentic, family-friendly and very relaxed.

## 4. Los Amantes

Known for its striking interior design and lively atmosphere. The Los Amantes terrace is the place to be for top-notch mezcal cocktails while the Oaxacan sky changes color.

- **Don't miss:** its menu of Oaxacan tapas and any house cocktail.
- **Vibe:** cosmopolitan, great music, ideal with friends.

![Bandstand lit with hundreds of warm string lights on a night in downtown Oaxaca](../../../assets/images/kiosco-iluminado-luces-calidas-noche-oaxaca.jpg)

## 5. Mezquite Gastropub

Just steps from Santo Domingo, Mezquite has a more laid-back feel, combining local ingredients with grilled dishes and comfort food.

- **Don't miss:** its steaks and gourmet tlayudas paired with local craft beer.
- **Vibe:** casual, great for a drink before a night out.

## 6. Gozobi

One of the largest and liveliest rooftops downtown. It's perfect for groups, with a wide menu ranging from Oaxacan dishes to international cuisine, plus live music several nights a week.

- **Don't miss:** its list of wild-agave mezcals.
- **Vibe:** festive, busy and energetic.

## 7. Praga

Strategically located right across from the Santo Domingo plaza. A great option to enjoy the colonial architecture from a front-row seat without paying for a fine-dining menu.

- **Don't miss:** a cold beer or an espadín mezcal at sunset while watching the plaza come alive.
- **Vibe:** easygoing, touristy and very photogenic.
